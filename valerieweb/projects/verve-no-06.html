Verve No. 06

Project Info

Hand-Tufted Wool

Year: 2026

Category: Rug Design

Palette: Cocoa, Moss, Sand

Verve No. 06 experiments with layered tuft heights to create subtle relief and shadow play. The motif logic was simplified to prioritize texture and rhythm over strict graphic contrast.

The piece was developed for hospitality lounge settings, where durability and visual warmth are equally critical in high-traffic use cases.
