Atlas No. 05

Project Info

Silk-Wool Blend

Year: 2026

Category: Rug Design

Palette: Sienna, Charcoal, Gold

Atlas No. 05 is built around denser border modules and a high-contrast center channel to anchor larger spaces. Silk accents were reserved for selected motifs to create controlled highlights.

The design direction balances archival references with cleaner contemporary spacing so the piece sits naturally in both classic and modern interiors.
