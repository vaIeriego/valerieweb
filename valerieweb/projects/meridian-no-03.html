Meridian No. 03

Project Info

Flat-weave

Year: 2024

Category: Rug Design

Palette: Brick, Navy, Wheat

Meridian No. 03 focuses on directional linework and balanced spacing. The woven structure was tuned to hold crisp geometric edges while keeping enough softness for residential interiors.

Its proportion system supports multiple room sizes by scaling the central axis and border density independently, preserving the original composition at each format.
