Nomad No. 01

Project Info

Hand-knotted Wool

Year: 2023

Category: Rug Design

Palette: Rust, Ochre, Ink

Nomad No. 01 explores historic border motifs through a denser knot map and a softened chroma profile. The central field is intentionally quiet, allowing the edge structure to do most of the visual movement.

The rug balances symmetry with slight irregularity so the design reads crafted rather than mechanical. Material contrast between matte wool areas and tighter knot zones gives the pattern depth under changing light.
