Soleil No. 04

Project Info

Tufted Wool Blend

Year: 2025

Category: Rug Design

Palette: Terracotta, Dust, Umber

Soleil No. 04 uses radial motif fragments to create a sense of movement without relying on strong contrast. The edge treatment intentionally softens toward corners to keep the composition open.

Material blend selection was driven by resilience and hand feel, targeting high-touch lounge spaces where softness and structure both matter.
