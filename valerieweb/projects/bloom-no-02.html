Bloom No. 02

Project Info

Printed Linen

Year: 2024

Category: Surface Print

Palette: Rose, Clay, Sand

Bloom No. 02 translates a botanical rhythm into repeat units designed for large-format rug applications. The print keeps edge continuity so adjoining panels feel uninterrupted in open spaces.

A muted tonal ladder supports layering with furniture and textiles while preserving graphic clarity up close. The result is soft from distance and intricate when viewed at hand scale.
