meet valerie gonzalez

Valerie Gonzalez is a print designer focused on rugs, textiles, and tactile pattern systems. Her work bridges contemporary composition with references to traditional weaving, color memory, and architectural rhythm.

With a process rooted in drawing, repeat studies, and material testing, she develops pieces that balance structure and softness while maintaining a strong visual identity across residential and editorial contexts.

For commissions, collaborations, and inquiries, contact: gonzalez@alumni.cooper.edu
